How to Reduce Restaurant Food Costs by 15-30%

Food costs crushing your margins? Here are 5 proven strategies with specific ROI:

1. Recipe Standardization (3-5% savings)

Document portions. Train staff. Use KMS for daily cost tracking.

2. Inventory Management (4-7% savings)

Weekly counts. FIFO systems. Par levels based on actual usage.

3. Vendor Negotiations (2-5% savings)

Compare 3+ vendors. Negotiate volume discounts. Review quarterly.

4. Menu Engineering (4-8% savings)

Analyze profitability vs popularity. Strategic pricing. Portion control.

5. Waste Reduction (2-5% savings)

Track waste by category. Root cause analysis. Staff training.

Combined impact: $75K-$300K annual savings for $1M revenue restaurant.

Building a Spirits Brand Sales System That Actually Works

Most spirits brands fail because they treat bars like retail stores. Here's the truth:

The Distribution Trap

Getting into distribution doesn't mean you're "in the market." It means you have expensive shelf space that nobody knows about.

The Velocity Game

Success = accounts × velocity. You need both. An account that doesn't sell is worse than no account at all.

What Actually Works:

  • Bartender education first: Not consumers. Bartenders sell your brand.
  • Focus on 10 accounts: Not 100. Dominate a few bars before expanding.
  • Build velocity programs: Staff incentives, signature serves, training sessions.
  • Track the right metrics: Depletion rates, not just placement.
  • Support your accounts: Marketing, training, events. Make them succeed.

The Rockey Method teaches exactly how to execute this strategy step-by-step.
